Voice of Customer Analyser

Type a brand name and it gathers that brand's public reviews from app stores and review sites, groups them into themes you approve, and shows what customers complain about and what they praise. The hard part is not collecting reviews. It is analysing them without paying a model to read every single one.

Open the app How it works

No sign-up and no API key needed. AI is optional, and you bring your own key.

1,200 Reviews for one brand from six store apps, collected in about 20 seconds.
9 Ways in: two app stores, Sitejabber, PissedConsumer, Reddit, Bluesky, YouTube comments, any page with review markup, and your own file.
0 AI calls needed. Sentiment, themes and classification all run without a model.

The hard part

AI only where it earns its place

Sending every review to a language model is slow, costs money on every run, and gives a different answer each time. So the model is the last step, not the first. Most of the work is done by rules you can read and change.

No AI

Sentiment from the stars

A one or two star review is negative and a four or five is positive. Posts with no rating, such as Reddit or Bluesky, and three star reviews are scored by a rule-based text scorer, one theme's sentences at a time, so "comfy shoes, awful delivery" is both.

You decide

Themes you approve

Keyword topics are suggested in about a second, or one AI call suggests named themes. You rename them, edit the keywords, approve, and save the list to reuse next month.

Optional

AI for the leftovers

Keywords place each review first, under every theme one of its sentences mentions. Only the reviews no keyword fits go to the model, 80 at a time, and every row records which method placed it.

Why it does not get round bot checks

Trustpilot, G2, Capterra, Yelp and Glassdoor block automated visitors. The app does not try to get past them. It says the site is blocked and asks for an export to upload instead, so every review in the dataset was collected the way the site allows.

Inside it

From a brand name to a report

Pick countries for the app stores, compare two periods or two brands on the same themes, read example quotes for each theme, and export the lot as an HTML report, Excel or CSV.

No AI

Fill the gaps yourself

The app lists the most common words in reviews no keyword placed. Pick a theme for any that belong and it adds them as keywords and re-sorts every review.

Any language

Other languages, counted

Each review's language is detected on the spot. Reviews the English keywords cannot read are counted apart, never forced into a wrong theme, and can be filtered out or sent to the model.

Month to month

Track a brand over time

App stores only keep recent reviews, so the app saves one file with every review so far and the approved themes. Load it next month and see which themes grew or turned more negative.
